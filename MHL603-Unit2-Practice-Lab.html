Chronology at a glance

1913 · Schoenberg*1958 · Varèse

Anchor the unit around 1923–24 (neoclassicism and machine music), 1948–52 (tape and total serialism), and 1955–58 (postwar ensemble and electronic works).

Exam-date note: Use 1913 for Schoenberg’s Op. 19, No. 6 because that is the date on the supplied study guide. The historically documented composition date is 1911.

Fast recognition wins

  • Wind octet only? Stravinsky’s Octet.
  • Sparse bell-like piano chords? Schoenberg Op. 19, No. 6.
  • Two hyper-pointillistic pianos? Boulez’s Structures 1a.
  • Mechanical pounding and sirens? Antheil’s Ballet Mécanique.
  • Actual train recordings? Schaeffer’s railway étude.
  • Sliding electronic choir? Messiaen’s Oraison.
